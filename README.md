# Keeper — A Groww Prototype for First-Time Gen Z Investors

Keeper is a feature concept designed for **Groww**, specifically targeting Gen Z first-time investors (ages 20–26) with irregular income (internships, freelancing, part-time roles, or first jobs).

### Core Insight
The barrier to investing isn't the minimum amount—it's the **confidence to commit money they might need back**. Keeper acts as a calm, reassuring decision layer between spare cash and the first investment, making starting feel **flexible, understandable, and reversible**.

---

### Home Screen Highlights
- **Single Phone-Width Screen**: Clean mobile-first container with warm, calm visual aesthetics.
- **Two Separated Balances**:
  - **Flexible money (₹1,200)**: Cash available anytime with zero lock-in for upcoming expenses or peace of mind.
  - **Invested (₹500)**: Long-term money quietly compounding for the future.
- **Single Prominent Action**: "Add money" primary CTA button.
- **Plain Language & Calm Tone**: No finance jargon, Bloomberg-style clutter, or stressful charts.
